# FocusLens v1.0.0: Foundations

The first release of FocusLens for Windows. It quietly tracks how you spend your time, understands what is on your screen, records and summarizes meetings, and lets you ask questions about your day using an AI model that can run entirely on your PC.

## Highlights

- **Local AI with Ollama.** Point FocusLens at your Ollama daemon and chat with your activity data without anything leaving your machine. Claude and OpenAI remain available if you prefer them.
- **Screen context.** FocusLens reads the text of your active window with the built-in Windows OCR engine so answers can reflect what you were actually working on. Images are not kept, and excluded apps and URLs are never captured.
- **Audio and meeting recording.** Meetings are detected automatically, audio is captured, and transcription runs offline on your PC. You get a summary with action items after the call.
- **Forest theme.** A refreshed look with a calm green palette, Georgia headings, pill buttons, and matching light and dark themes.
- **Automatic updates.** FocusLens checks GitHub Releases in the background, downloads updates quietly, and applies them when you restart.

## Also in this release

- Streaming AI chat with conversation history and markdown rendering.
- Google sign-in through Supabase.
- Privacy controls for pausing tracking and excluding apps or URLs.
- A tag-driven release pipeline that builds the installer and update feed.

## Known issues

- Offline transcription uses the Windows desktop speech recognizer, which is less accurate than Whisper. Better engines are planned, and it needs a speech recognizer for your language to be installed.
- Screen context OCR depends on the OCR languages installed for your Windows user profile.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, 64-bit
- Optional: [Ollama](https://ollama.com) for fully local AI

## Install

Download `FocusLens-win-Setup.exe` from the assets below and run it. The installer includes the .NET runtime and starts the background agent for you.
