# FocusLens v1.2.0: Focus tools

Focus Mode arrives on Windows. Tell FocusLens what you are working on, and it notices when you drift, nudges you, and if you stay away, closes or blocks the distraction. It is built to stay light, even on laptops with integrated graphics.

## Highlights

- **Focus Mode.** A new Focus page in the sidebar asks three things: your goal ("study .NET"), how long, and what should happen when you drift (close it, or block it). Your last choice is remembered.
- **A patience bar, not a hair trigger.** While you are off topic, a bar drains (45 seconds by default, adjustable from 15 to 180). Halfway, you get a nudge and a sound. When it empties, the tab or window is closed after a short "Closing in 5" warning with a "Stay 5 min" option, or covered by an overlay until you close it. Going back to your work refills the bar, and the overlay always has an "End focus session" button.
- **A floating widget and a tray timer.** A small widget that stays on top shows your live focus score, your goal, the time left and the patience bar. Drag it anywhere; it remembers where you put it. Warnings slide out underneath it. The tray icon shows the minutes left, and its menu has "End focus session". Either can be turned off; warnings always appear.
- **Session history with insights.** Every session is saved with its score, focused and distracted time, a score-over-time chart, the sites and apps that pulled you away, and each distraction in order.
- **Private by default.** Your goal is compared with the window title and address by the model you chose in Settings > AI. With Ollama, nothing leaves your PC. If the model cannot decide, nothing is closed or blocked.

## Light on your PC

- **Checks every 6 seconds, and you choose.** Settings > Focus > Performance (and "Keep it light" on the Focus page) lets you check the window every 2 to 30 seconds. Switching windows is noticed right away regardless, and the patience bar still moves every second.
- **Pauses when you are away.** No checks after a minute without keyboard or mouse input, or while the PC is locked or asleep. The bar does not drain while the PC is locked.
- **Glances are free.** A new page is only sent to the AI after it has stayed in front for 2 seconds, and each page is judged once per session.
- **Short answers from the AI.** Focus checks ask for a one-word answer with reasoning turned off, so a local model spends a fraction of a second per page instead of seconds.
- **Minimal visual effects.** Turn off the widget's shadow and let the clock tick once a minute. Automatic does this while battery saver is on or Windows animations are turned off.
- **A separate model for focus checks.** Optionally use a small Ollama model (such as llama3.2:1b) just for Focus Mode, while chat keeps your larger one.

## Also in this release

- An allowlist of apps and sites that are never treated as a distraction (for example "code, learn.microsoft.com"). Apps can be named by their display name or program name.
- Words from your goal found in a window title count as on topic straight away, without asking the AI.
- Reading a browser's address runs in the background with a time limit, so FocusLens stays responsive when a browser is busy, for example while playing a video.
- Windows' own surfaces (the desktop, taskbar, Start, File Explorer and Settings) are never treated as a distraction.

## Fixes

- The Focus page, Settings > Focus and the recent chats list no longer fail to load. A build check now catches a missing style before release.
- The "Start focus" and "Close this window" buttons show their labels again.
- "Close this window" on the block overlay now closes the window. It used to fail because clicking the overlay moved the app out of the way.
- Closing an app that asks "Save changes?" no longer counts as a second distraction while you answer.
- The block overlay shrinks to fit small windows instead of cutting off its buttons.
- The dashboard refreshes every 30 seconds instead of showing numbers up to 15 minutes old.
- The tracking agent no longer stops at start-up on Windows editions without screen text recognition (N editions, Server, or no OCR language installed). It falls back to reading the window's text.
- Guide: pressing Enter in the request box now starts the guide, and a guide that cannot read the screen ends with a message instead of waiting on "Thinking..." forever.
- Settings > Permissions shows the tracking agent as running once it has started, instead of "not running" from the check made at launch.

## Known issues

- Tabs are closed by sending Ctrl+W to the browser while it is in front. If you are holding Shift, Alt or the Windows key at that moment, nothing is closed, to avoid closing the whole window.
- Windows does not let FocusLens close or read apps running as administrator, so those windows are never closed. You are told when a close fails.
- Browser tabs are recognised through the address bar. Arc and Opera have not been verified, and a browser in full-screen or kiosk mode may hide its address, in which case only the title is judged.
- Moving to a different tab in the same browser is noticed at the next check (up to your chosen interval), so the bar may drain for a few seconds after you leave a distracting tab.
- The block overlay covers the window but does not pause audio or video playing underneath.
- Turning the display off without locking the PC is not detected as away until a minute passes without input.
- A session in progress is saved as "ended early" when you quit FocusLens from the tray. If FocusLens crashes, the session in progress is lost; finished sessions are kept.
- With reasoning models that cannot turn reasoning off, focus checks may come back undecided, and nothing is enforced. A small non-reasoning model for focus checks avoids this.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, 64-bit
- Optional: [Ollama](https://ollama.com) for fully local AI. FocusLens can install it for you.

## Upgrading

Existing installs update automatically in the background and apply the update on restart.
